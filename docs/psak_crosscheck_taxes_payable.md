# PSAK cross-check: `taxes_payable` (rule D-03)

**Date:** 22 Oct 2026. **Checked by:** Claude (AI). **Status:** AI-checked against the PSAK text
supplied by Gail (`PSAK_212.pdf`, IAI 2024). Human verification is still open (see §4).

## 1. The rule being checked

D-03 (approved by Gail, 19 Oct 2026; workbook `Mapping_Decisions`):
`taxes_payable` = corporate income tax payable only (PPh badan, e.g. Art. 25/29 of the group
entities). Withholding taxes (PPh 21, 22, 23, 26, 4(2)), VAT (PPN), local and other taxes and
deferred tax are excluded. Total "utang pajak" is not a substitute.

## 2. What PSAK 212 says (paraphrased, paragraph references)

| PSAK 212 | Content | Bearing on D-03 |
|---|---|---|
| ¶2 Scope | Income taxes are all domestic and foreign taxes **based on taxable profit** (plus withholding taxes payable by subsidiaries/associates on distributions to the reporting entity) | Defines what counts as "income tax" |
| ¶5 Definition | Current tax: income tax payable (recoverable) on the taxable profit (loss) of a period | The payable in question is current tax |
| ¶12 | Current tax for current and prior periods, **to the extent unpaid**, is recognised as a liability; an overpayment is an asset | The liability = unpaid current tax |
| ¶71–73 | Current tax assets and liabilities are offset only with a legally enforceable right and intent to settle net; across group entities only under the same conditions | Presentation may be net; affects comparability across years |
| DK01, DK03 | PSAK 212 removes the earlier provisions on final income tax (*pajak final*); final tax is imposed on gross amounts, not on taxable profit | Final tax is outside the income-tax scope |

## 3. Result

| Component (SIDO FY2021 tax note, PDF p62–63) | Under PSAK 212 | D-03 treatment | Consistent? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPh Pasal 29 (Company 99.237; subsidiaries 18.133) | Unpaid current tax for the year (¶12) | Included | Yes |
| PPh Pasal 25 (Company 29.809; subsidiaries 2.162) | Unpaid instalment of the current year's corporate income tax: also unpaid current tax (¶12) | Included | Yes |
| PPh Pasal 21, 22, 23, 26 | Taxes withheld from payments to others: the entity owes them as withholding agent; they are not tax on the entity's own taxable profit (outside ¶2) | Excluded | Yes |
| PPh Pasal 4(2) | Final tax or withholding on gross amounts, not on taxable profit (¶2; DK01, DK03) | Excluded | Yes |
| Pajak Pertambahan Nilai (PPN) | Not an income tax | Excluded | Yes |
| **Pajak Luar Negeri (Company: 1 in 2021; "-" in 2020)** | If it is a foreign tax **based on taxable profit**, ¶2 includes it in income tax. The note does not say what it is | Excluded in the draft | **Open (REVIEW).** Immaterial here (Rp1 million against 149.341), but the rule should say what to do |
| Claims for tax refund ("Taksiran tagihan pengembalian pajak", other non-current assets) | Current tax asset (¶12), presented separately, not offset (¶71) | Not netted | Yes |

**Conclusion:** D-03 as approved is consistent with PSAK 212 ¶2, ¶5 and ¶12 for every
component of the SIDO FY2021 note except one open point: foreign taxes whose nature is not stated.

**Proposed clarification of D-03 (PROPOSED, not approved):** include a foreign tax only when
the note identifies it as income tax on taxable profit; otherwise exclude it, flag REVIEW, and
record the amount.

**Consistency with Beneish (1999):** the model subtracts the change in *income taxes payable*
(the current portion) from the change in current liabilities. Taking corporate current tax only
matches that definition; total "utang pajak" would not.

## 4. Limits and open verification

- **Standard in force for FY2021.** PSAK 212 is effective from 1 Jan 2024 (renumbering of
  PSAK 46). SIDO FY2021 was prepared under PSAK 46. That PSAK 46 contained the same ¶2, ¶5,
  ¶12 and ¶71–73 at the time is **assumed, not verified** (only the PSAK 212 text was supplied).
- **Human verification needed** of: (a) the paragraph reading above; (b) the PSAK 46 equivalents
  for FY2020–FY2023 reports; (c) the treatment of "Pajak Luar Negeri".
- Other golden fields (right-of-use assets under PSAK 73/116, receivables net of ECL under
  PSAK 71/109, revenue under PSAK 72/115, leases in debt) are **not** cross-checked: those
  standards were not supplied.
